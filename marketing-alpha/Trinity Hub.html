Trinity / 01 — The three plugins

Three plugins. One operating layer for WordPress and AI.

Each one is a finished product that solves a specific problem on its own. Installed together they become the operating layer — the way your AI client and your WordPress site speak the same language without anything in between.

The Bridge · Node.js
Abilities MCP
Connect any AI client to any number of WordPress sites — from one place, on your machine.
Runs locally. Holds your keys in the OS keychain. Routes a single Claude or Cursor or Codex session across every site you operate. New site? One command. Multisite? Subsites included.

The Gate · WordPress Plugin
MCP Adapter
Turns your site into an AI-callable surface — with consent, scope, and safety baked in.
OAuth 2.1 with scoped keys, not pasted passwords. A consent screen the operator actually sees. Three-bucket redaction so secrets, payment data and contact PII never leak into chat. Every gate is named when it says no.
