Abilities for Fluent / 02 — In one breath

What it is. Why it exists. How it works. What it brings you.

A reading of this plugin before any feature list. Same shape as every product in the Trinity — value first, spec second.

What
A WordPress plugin that registers every Fluent surface as a callable ability.
Contacts, segments, automations, forms, communities, courses, products, orders, tickets, bookings, boards, affiliates — each surface becomes a named ability the AI can run.
Why
So your AI doesn't stop at WordPress core.
Most operators don't run the business on core alone — they run it on the suite. Without this plugin, the AI knows your posts but not your contacts. With it, the operating layer reaches the surfaces you actually live in.
How
Conditional. Loads only what you have. Inherits the same gate.
FluentCRM installed? CRM abilities register. FluentBooking installed? Booking abilities register. Same Adapter, same scopes, same redaction — no second control panel to learn.
What it brings
A full sovereign suite for any business on WordPress.
Trinity + Fluent + WordPress core = everything you need to run a business or a project venture, AI-operable, on open source you own. No SaaS in the middle. No data leaving the site.

Abilities for Fluent / 04 — In practice

What it looks like in your day.

Two scenes from the alpha. Real sequences the AI can run today, on a site running the Trinity and Fluent together. The shape of the operator's day after.

Scene 01 · CRM & campaigns
A campaign retro before lunch.
"Look at last month's campaign. Pull the contacts who replied. Segment them by behaviour. Draft tomorrow's follow-up matched to each segment."
The AI reads the campaign, runs the segments through FluentCRM, drafts the messages, attaches the right tags, queues nothing without your approval. The retro stops being a Saturday job.
"What used to take an afternoon takes a conversation."
Scene 02 · Support & cart
A ticket arrives. The AI is already reading it.
"Customer wrote in. Find their prior orders, check if they're on a current plan, draft a reply with the right knowledge-base article."
FluentSupport reads the ticket, FluentCart returns the order history, FluentCRM returns the lifecycle state. The AI writes the reply. You read it. You hit send — or you don't. The boundary stays human.
"The dashboard becomes the review layer, not the work layer."
